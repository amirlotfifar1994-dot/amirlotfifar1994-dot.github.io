PVD vs aluminum anodizing

Compare PVD and aluminum anodizing for decorative parts: matte/satin finish, color, durability, and cost.

Consultation & sample review

Which makes more sense for aluminum?

Anodizing is a common route for compatible aluminum alloys; PVD is evaluated when a special metallic finish or different surface structure is required. Selection depends on alloy, preparation and project criteria.

CriteriaAnodizingPVD
Best for aluminumCommon route for compatible aluminum alloysWorth evaluating with compatible preparation and substrate
Special colorsMore limitedWorth evaluating for selected special metallic ranges
Matte/satin feelMatte/satin finishes are common optionsPossible depending on finish
Scratch protectionGood depending on typeDepends on coating stack, preparation and contact conditions

FAQ

Can anodizing and PVD be combined?

Sometimes possible, but requires compatibility testing and sampling.

Before concluding

What data closes this comparison?

The comparison table is only a starting point. These four inputs determine whether the conclusion applies to your real part.

01

Substrate and current coating

Alloy, surface condition, previous plating/paint and whether re-preparation is possible.

02

Geometry and critical zones

Recesses, edges, threads, sealing zones, masking areas and sensitive tolerances.

03

Service conditions

Moisture, cleaners, touch, wear, temperature or any important functional load.

04

Acceptance criterion

Reference color/texture, thickness, critical-area appearance or a test that closes the decision.

For aluminum, the alloy is part of the decision

Anodizing is formed on the aluminum itself, so alloy series, previous processing, surface texture and tolerance can affect appearance and process route. If the alloy is unknown, the comparison remains preliminary.

A visual sample does not replace technical data

Two parts with similar appearance may come from very different processes. To choose between PVD and anodizing, define the substrate, service environment and dimensional limits in addition to the color/texture reference.

What should you do after comparing?

If one or more inputs above are unknown, it is too early for a final choice. Read the relevant guide or send the part details for review.
