Before comparing

Three inputs that can change the comparison

01

Actual substrate

Alloy, previous coating and current surface.

02

Environment and contact

Moisture, wear, cleaners, temperature or hand contact.

03

Acceptance criterion

Color/texture, tolerance or the test that closes the decision.

This matrix is for initial decision-making and does not mean Kavian provides every comparison method listed here.

MethodWorth evaluating whenKey constraintNext step
PVDControlled color/finish or a defined surface requirement on a compatible partSubstrate, line-of-sight, preparation and acceptance criteria must be clearReview sample and part specification
