Our operating principles
01

Clarity before quotation

We do not turn incomplete information into a firm number; part risks are reviewed first.

02

Sample before generalization

A sample or photo result is not generalized to every substrate or production run.

03

Defined acceptance

Visual or technical acceptance criteria are clarified before production where possible.

Professional boundaries

Trust is stronger when the limits of each answer are clear

Part of surface engineering is recognizing the limits of the available information. A photo, a sample and the actual part provide different levels of confidence and should not be treated as interchangeable.

01

What can a photo tell us?

Overall geometry, visible surface condition, target finish and some visible risks can support initial screening, but not determine the final process outcome.

02

When does a sample matter?

When color matching, premium appearance, a specific substrate or repeat orders are sensitive, a sample can narrow the gap between an imagined expectation and a visible reference.

03

What requires the real project?

Final feasibility, preparation route and appropriate control depth depend on the substrate, surface condition, application and acceptance criteria of that project.
