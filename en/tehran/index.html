FAQ about PVD services in Tehran

For architectural and industrial projects, the right coating directly affects durability, color, and final quality.

How long does PVD coating take for Tehran orders?
Turnaround depends on the part type, surface preparation, and quantity—typically a few business days up to about a week. Send photos and specs for an exact estimate.
How do pickup and delivery work inside Tehran?
Delivery inside Tehran can be arranged via courier/freight. You can also coordinate an in-person handoff during business hours.
Which colors are most popular for luxury projects in Tehran?
Gold, rose gold, smoky (gunmetal), and matte/gloss black are popular for luxury projects, chosen based on style and application.
What information is needed for a PVD price quote?
Part type and material (steel/brass/aluminum), quantity, target color, dimensions, photos of the surface condition, and where it will be used (indoor/outdoor) help us quote accurately.
