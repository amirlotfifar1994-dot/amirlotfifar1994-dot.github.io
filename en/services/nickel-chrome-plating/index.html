FAQ

What parts are suitable for nickel-chrome plating?

Nickel-chrome can suit parts targeting a bright finish and corrosion control, but pretreatment, layer structure, and acceptance criteria should be defined for the actual substrate and service conditions.

How do you control plating quality?

QC typically includes visual checks (brightness/stains), uniformity, and when required, thickness and adhesion checks. Proper prep and polishing are key to the final result.

How do you prevent stains or peeling?

By thorough cleaning/degreasing, suitable pretreatment, controlled bath parameters and stepwise rinsing. If the part had a previous coating, telling us helps reduce risk.
