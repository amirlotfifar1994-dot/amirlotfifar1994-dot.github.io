Items to check before coating faucets and hardware

  • Appearance and reflectivity should be matched to a reference sample and the actual substrate condition.
  • Finish behavior under moisture and daily contact depends on preparation, coating structure, and care.
  • Threads, sealing seats, and assembly interfaces should be identified for masking before coating.
  • Batch color consistency is managed with a reference sample and process control, not by the color name alone.

FAQ

Is faucet PVD suitable for humid environments?

PVD suitability for wet environments depends on the substrate, preparation, coating stack, sealing interfaces, and use conditions; sensitive projects should use samples and defined acceptance criteria.

Will cleaners change the color?

Cleaner resistance depends on the coating system and the cleaner itself. Strong acidic/alkaline or abrasive products can damage the finish and should be aligned with the project care guidance.
