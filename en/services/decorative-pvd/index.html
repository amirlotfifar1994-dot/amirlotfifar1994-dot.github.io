Decorative PVD decision matrix

For decorative work, the final appearance comes from color, gloss level, surface preparation and the approval reference. A color name alone is not enough.

01

Define the visual reference

  • Target color and reference sample
  • Gloss, matte, satin or mirror direction
  • Base-surface quality and texture
02

  • Variation between parts and polishing condition
  • Lighting and color-comparison method
  • Need for a sample or pilot batch
03

Prepare the project brief

  • Part photos and substrate
  • Quantity and approximate dimensions
  • Finish photo or physical reference

Why PVD for luxury work?

  • Uniform look and clean finish (gloss or matte)
  • With suitable preparation and coating structure, surface resistance and finish stability can be targeted for the intended use.
  • Popular colors: gold, rose gold, smoky, black, silver

FAQ

What protection is recommended for decorative parts?

If a clear protective layer is needed, it should be selected around contact level, cleaners, and the project acceptance criteria; its benefit should preferably be verified on a sample.

How do you reduce batch-to-batch color variation?

To reduce batch color variation, the reference sample, surface preparation, fixturing, and process parameters should be defined for that project and controlled during production.
