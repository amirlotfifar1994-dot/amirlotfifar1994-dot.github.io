Fast troubleshooting path

These three checks help you root-cause most visual issues quickly.

  • Contamination: proper cleaning and drying.
  • Polish/substrate: any waviness will be amplified after coating.
  • Outgassing/moisture: common in porous or cast parts.

Streaks and stains: what they usually signal

Linear streaks and dark spots often come from localized contamination, cleaner residue, or poor fixture contact.

If the pattern repeats, inspect the handling path and the fixture.

Pinholes and micro-voids

Pinholes can be caused by outgassing, casting porosity, or dust particles.

Typical fixes: better prep, full drying, and a suitable interlayer when needed.

Checklist before repeating the run

Coat a control sample with standard pretreatment to see whether the root cause is the part or the process.

Record parameters and cleaning steps so defects become reproducible and removable.

Frequently asked questions

If only some parts are affected, what does that mean?

It usually points to substrate or cleaning variability on those parts: contamination, porosity, or machining oils.

Does rework always solve it?

Not if the root cause (contamination/polish/outgassing) remains. Rework just increases cost and is rarely stable.
