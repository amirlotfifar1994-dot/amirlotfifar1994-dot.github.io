Common finishes and where they fit

  • Gold & champagne: popular for architectural hardware and luxury décor.
  • Rose gold: used in premium interiors and warm-material palettes.
  • Smoke/black: suited to modern, minimal architectural styles.
  • Silver/steel: clean industrial look matching stainless parts.

Glossy vs. matte

Glossy finishes look more premium but can show micro-scratches. Matte reduces reflections and fingerprints and works well for high-traffic or minimal designs.

Selection tips for architectural & industrial projects

  • In humid areas, be stricter about cleaning and maintenance conditions.
  • For high wear, focus on the system and prep—not only the color.
  • Check samples under the project’s real lighting.

Proper care and cleaning

  • Avoid abrasive cleaners or rough pads.
  • Use a soft cloth and mild detergent.
  • For stubborn stains, consult before using aggressive chemicals.

FAQ

Which finish is most common for architectural hardware?

Gold, champagne, and smoke are common choices. The best pick depends on style, lighting, and hand-contact frequency.

Does smoke finish stain easily?

Maintenance matters. Soft cloth + mild cleaners usually work well; avoid abrasive chemicals.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a quote?

Send dimensions, material, color/finish, quantity, and use conditions (wear/humidity/cleaners) so we can estimate more accurately.

How long does an order take?

Lead time depends on substrate, quantity, and the production queue. Share your project details for a precise schedule.

How do you evaluate coating durability and quality?

Depending on the project, we use adhesion and wear tests plus visual inspection; environmental/corrosion checks are added when needed. Sampling before mass production is recommended.

Do you provide color sampling for luxury projects?

Yes. For gold/rose-gold/smoke/rainbow, we prepare a master color sample to reduce batch variation.

What affects pricing?

Surface/size, substrate, color/finish, quantity, prep needs, and topcoat are main factors.

What is the typical lead time?

Depends on volume and prep route. For exact timing, we need the sample and project specs.
