Brass polishing essentials

These simple habits reduce variation in production.

  • Use suitable compounds and pads to avoid waviness.
  • Clean between steps so residues do not stay on the surface.
  • Take final drying and soft packaging seriously.

Why brass stains easily

Brass can be more sensitive to certain residues and handling, leaving visible marks faster.

Staged cleaning and controlled handling are key.

Uniform polishing without waves

Waviness often comes from excessive pressure or the wrong pad. Consistent motion and tools help.

On flat parts, pressure and time control matter a lot.

Ready for PVD or plating

After polishing, the surface must be fully clean and dry to protect adhesion and appearance.

For premium coatings, do not skip sampling.

Frequently asked questions

Should brass be PVD-coated or plated?

It depends on target color, use conditions, and budget. Brass can work with both, if the substrate is prepared correctly.

How do we avoid scratches in packaging?

Use soft packaging, separators between parts, and avoid metal-to-metal contact during transport.
