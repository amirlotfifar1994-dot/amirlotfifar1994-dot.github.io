Introduction

PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) forms thin, uniform films that can deliver premium finishes (gold, rose-gold, smoke, etc.) and practical wear/corrosion resistance—depending on the system and preparation. Kavian Coating (Tehran) provides PVD for industrial parts, architectural hardware, and decorative projects.

Benefits

  • Consistent color and diverse finishes (gold, rose gold, smoke, etc.).
  • Good resistance against wear and scratches in many applications.
  • Controlled thickness and repeatability for batch projects.

Applications

  • Architectural hardware: handles, fittings, profiles, and decorative parts.
  • Industrial parts: machinery components, tools, and surfaces needing enhancement.
  • Luxury finishes: decorative parts with premium look and special colors.

Selection tips

  • If appearance and color variety are your top priority, PVD is often the primary option.
  • For very high wear or harsh conditions, also consider ceramic or heavy-duty systems.
  • Surface prep (cleaning/polishing) directly affects the final result.

FAQ

FAQ

Which parts are suitable for PVD?

For parts where appearance and color uniformity matter (hardware, décor, architectural parts) and many industrial components needing surface enhancement.

Will PVD colors stay stable?

With the right coating choice and proper care, color stability is high; environment and usage affect longevity.

Is polishing required before coating?

For luxury finishes, preparation and polishing often improve results. For industrial needs, a different prep method may be more appropriate.

Need help choosing?

Contact us to choose the right coating (PVD or ceramic) based on material, use case, and desired look.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a quote?

Send dimensions, material, color/finish, quantity, and use conditions (wear/humidity/cleaners) so we can estimate more accurately.

How long does an order take?

Lead time depends on substrate, quantity, and the production queue. Share your project details for a precise schedule.

How do you evaluate coating durability and quality?

Depending on the project, we use adhesion and wear tests plus visual inspection; environmental/corrosion checks are added when needed. Sampling before mass production is recommended.

Do you provide color sampling for luxury projects?

Yes. For gold/rose-gold/smoke/rainbow, we prepare a master color sample to reduce batch variation.

What affects pricing?

Surface/size, substrate, color/finish, quantity, prep needs, and topcoat are main factors.

What is the typical lead time?

Depends on volume and prep route. For exact timing, we need the sample and project specs.

Frequently asked questions

Which parts are suitable for PVD coating?

It can suit faucets, hardware, decorative accessories, and other parts where appearance and the agreed performance criteria matter.

How does PVD differ from conventional electroplating?

PVD is a family of vacuum-deposition processes; final performance depends on substrate, preparation, coating system, and service conditions, so no universal advantage is assumed for every project.

How should a PVD-coated surface be maintained?

Use a soft cloth and mild cleaner; avoid abrasive pads and strong acidic or chlorine-based chemicals.
