Why fingerprints show on PVD

On glossy finishes and darker colors, natural skin oils are more visible—this doesn’t mean low quality.

What matters is using the right cleaning method and avoiding abrasive cloths or harsh chemicals.

Quick daily cleaning

A soft microfiber cloth + lukewarm water is enough. Then dry with a clean cloth.

  • Avoid wiping dry over dust (micro-scratch risk).
  • For oily smudges, use a tiny amount of mild soap and rinse thoroughly.

What to avoid

  • Bleach, acid/alkaline cleaners and abrasive powders.
  • Scouring pads and steel wool.
  • Unknown multi-purpose sprays (test a hidden spot first).

Pro tip for luxury projects

If the part is in a high-touch area, matte or brushed finishes typically show fewer fingerprints.

FAQ

FAQ

Can I use glass cleaner?

If it’s ammonia-free and non-abrasive and you rinse/wipe quickly, it’s usually fine. Do a spot test first.

Why do I get streaks after cleaning?

Usually leftover cleaner or the wrong cloth. Rinse well and dry with a clean microfiber cloth.

What’s the best cloth for PVD?

A soft, clean microfiber cloth. Rough/dirty cloths can cause micro-scratches.
