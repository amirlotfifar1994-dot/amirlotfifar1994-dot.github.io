Frequently asked questions

What matters most when choosing color (gold/rose-gold/silver)?

Base material, application, contact level, environment, and finish all matter. A reference sample, surface preparation, and acceptance criteria should be defined before production.

Why is polishing before coating so important?

Polishing directly affects texture, light reflection, and how surface defects appear. Final coating quality depends on suitable preparation of the actual substrate.

Which articles are most useful for service projects?

Start with the selection guide, method comparisons, and quality-control articles; then go deeper based on substrate, service conditions, and target finish.
