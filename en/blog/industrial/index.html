Frequently asked questions

What is the most important step before industrial coating?

Surface preparation (degreasing, proper roughness, and full cleanliness) has the biggest impact on adhesion and durability and is the main source of defects when done poorly.

How do we reduce defects like peeling or blisters?

Standardize humidity, cleanliness, oil contamination control, and roughness profile. Then lock the process with QC tests such as adhesion and thickness before mass production.

Which method is better for high durability?

It depends on the substrate, operating environment, and cost. For some cases powder or e-coat is better; in others PVD with proper prep is the best choice.
