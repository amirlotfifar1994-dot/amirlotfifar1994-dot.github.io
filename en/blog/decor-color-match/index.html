Gold & rose-gold: where do they shine?

Gold is a common choice in warm kitchens and classic styles.

Rose-gold pairs nicely with light stone, warm woods, and modern-luxury interiors.

Smoky & black: the modern pick

Smoky tones match stainless and neutral palettes and are common in modern interiors.

Black (especially matte) works well for high contrast and minimalist design.

Take lighting & polishing seriously

The same color can look different under warm/cool lighting and on gloss vs matte surfaces.

For sensitive projects, selecting via a sample in the real environment gives the best results.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a quote?

Send dimensions, material, color/finish, quantity, and use conditions (wear/humidity/cleaners) so we can estimate more accurately.

How long does an order take?

Lead time depends on substrate, quantity, and the production queue. Share your project details for a precise schedule.

How do you evaluate coating durability and quality?

Depending on the project, we use adhesion and wear tests plus visual inspection; environmental/corrosion checks are added when needed. Sampling before mass production is recommended.

Do you provide color sampling for luxury projects?

Yes. For gold/rose-gold/smoke/rainbow, we prepare a master color sample to reduce batch variation.

Is sampling necessary?

For luxury or volume projects, sampling reduces risk of color variation and defects.

Any key care tip?

Avoid abrasives and very strong cleaners; use a soft cloth.
