Color vs finish: a common misunderstanding

Glossy/matte/brushed finishes change reflectivity and scratch visibility—so perceived durability differs.

For luxury projects, picking the right finish is as important as the color itself.

3 key factors behind color durability

  • Surface preparation (polish, cleaning, substrate uniformity).
  • Coating process control (parameters and consistency).
  • Environment & usage (humidity, cleaners, frequent touch).

Recommendations for gold/rose gold/smoked

Darker tones show fingerprints more; matte or brushed can be a smarter choice.

For luxury projects, sampling before production reduces color mismatch risk.

Proper care means longer-lasting appearance

Cleaning with a soft cloth and mild detergent preserves shine and prevents chemical damage.

Avoid acidic/alkaline and abrasive products—these cause the most damage.

FAQ

FAQ

Is PVD suitable for outdoor use?

It depends on the part and environment. Outdoor use requires evaluating the usage scenario and required resistance.

Why can the color differ between sample and batch?

Substrate, polish and process parameters can shift tone. Sampling and QC control solve this.

Which finish is best for luxury projects?

For high-touch areas, matte/brushed is often better. Glossy looks premium but needs more careful maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a quote?

Send dimensions, material, color/finish, quantity, and use conditions (wear/humidity/cleaners) so we can estimate more accurately.

How long does an order take?

Lead time depends on substrate, quantity, and the production queue. Share your project details for a precise schedule.

How do you evaluate coating durability and quality?

Depending on the project, we use adhesion and wear tests plus visual inspection; environmental/corrosion checks are added when needed. Sampling before mass production is recommended.

Do you provide color sampling for luxury projects?

Yes. For gold/rose-gold/smoke/rainbow, we prepare a master color sample to reduce batch variation.

What affects pricing?

Surface/size, substrate, color/finish, quantity, prep needs, and topcoat are main factors.

What is the typical lead time?

Depends on volume and prep route. For exact timing, we need the sample and project specs.
