Why is PVD popular in luxury markets?

Uniform appearance, color variety, and the choice of gloss or matte make PVD a common option for faucets and hardware.

However, the final result is not only about the coating—surface preparation and substrate quality are critical.

Substrate & surface preparation

To achieve a luxury finish, polishing/roughness must be controlled and contamination removed.

If the part had prior plating/paint, knowing that layer helps choose the right preparation path.

Choosing the finish: gloss or matte?

Gloss looks more “luxury” and reflective but shows fingerprints more.

Matte is often better for high-touch areas, modern designs, and reduced reflections.

More accurate ordering: color sampling

For decorative projects, sampling and approval before full batch reduces color mismatch risk.

Sharing a reference sample or accurate target-color photo helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a quote?

Send dimensions, material, color/finish, quantity, and use conditions (wear/humidity/cleaners) so we can estimate more accurately.

How long does an order take?

Lead time depends on substrate, quantity, and the production queue. Share your project details for a precise schedule.

How do you evaluate coating durability and quality?

Depending on the project, we use adhesion and wear tests plus visual inspection; environmental/corrosion checks are added when needed. Sampling before mass production is recommended.

Do you provide color sampling for luxury projects?

Yes. For gold/rose-gold/smoke/rainbow, we prepare a master color sample to reduce batch variation.

What affects pricing?

Surface/size, substrate, color/finish, quantity, prep needs, and topcoat are main factors.

What is the typical lead time?

Depends on volume and prep route. For exact timing, we need the sample and project specs.

Frequently asked questions

Is gloss or matte PVD better for faucets?

Gloss can look more reflective but tends to show fingerprints more readily; matte finishes can be easier to keep visually uniform.

How does PVD perform against moisture and cleaners?

Performance against moisture and mild cleaners depends on the substrate, preparation, coating system, and service conditions; sampling or testing is recommended for sensitive projects.

How can PVD-coated faucets be maintained for longer service life?

Gentle cleaning, drying after washing, and avoiding abrasive materials help preserve the agreed finish.
