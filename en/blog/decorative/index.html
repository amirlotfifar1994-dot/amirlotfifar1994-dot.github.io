Frequently asked questions

What is the best color for luxury faucets?

Gold or rosegold are common for a luxury look, but the right choice depends on lighting, base material, and touch frequency. For a mirror gloss finish, surface preparation and polishing are critical.

Matte or gloss?

Gloss looks more premium but shows fingerprints and micro-scratches more. Matte controls glare and smudges better and is often safer for high-traffic areas.

How should we care for decorative PVD parts?

Avoid strong acidic/alkaline cleaners. A soft cloth and mild cleaner are sufficient. If exposed to chemicals, choose the finish and protective top coat accordingly.
